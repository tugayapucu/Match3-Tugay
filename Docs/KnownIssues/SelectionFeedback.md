# Selection feedback

Status: Resolved in Phase 15. Verified in tests and with a real finger on a Galaxy
S23 (Android 16). That is one device and one person's judgement, not every Android
device, screen size or player.

## Original problem

After a tap selected a tile, nothing on screen changed. A non-adjacent tap kept the
original selection silently, so a new attempt could look unresponsive. On touch,
taps felt ignored. The Phase 13 audit confirmed it on the S23: the screen was
pixel-identical after a first tap and after an invalid tap.

## The cue

- **What it looks like:** four white corner brackets with a thin dark outline,
  drawn over the selected tile.
- **Where the art comes from:** original artwork in
  `Assets/Art/Tiles/PrismaticEnergy/SelectionHighlight.png`, generated by
  `Docs/Art/GenerateSelectionHighlight.py`. Provenance and import settings are in
  `Docs/Art/README.md`.
- **How it is drawn:** `Tile.prefab` has a hidden child, `SelectionHighlight`: a
  SpriteRenderer one sorting order above the gem, with no collider.
  `TileView.SetSelected` only switches that child on or off. The tile's sprite,
  scale, tint and tap area never change.
- **Who decides when it shows:** `BoardInputController.RefreshSelectionCue`. The cue
  shows only while a tile is selected, no move is running and the controller is
  enabled. It is hidden before any swap animation starts. It is cleared after a
  move, Restart, a win or loss, and a dead-board recovery re-render.
- **Wiring check:** `BoardView.ValidateConfiguration` fails at level start if the
  highlight is missing, has no sprite or is not a direct child.

## Selection rules

**What changed in Phase 15:**

- **Timing.** A tap now commits on release, once it is known not to be a swipe.
  It used to commit on press. This is the approved tap/swipe classification.
- **One new rule, approved during Step 10.** Tapping the already selected tile
  again cancels the selection. It spends no move and starts no swap.

**What is unchanged:**

- a non-adjacent, outside or Empty tap keeps the selection;
- a tap on an adjacent tile attempts the swap through the existing path;
- the selection clears once that move resolves or the invalid swap rolls back.

Swipes use the same swap path. A rejected swipe keeps the existing selection.

## Validation

- **Tests:**
  - `SelectionHighlightTests` (EditMode);
  - `BoardInputControllerSelectionTests` and the cue assertions in
    `BoardBootstrapTests` (PlayMode);
  - the Step 13 full regression.
- **S23, real finger (Step 10):**
  - the cue was reported "clearly different" from Nova's permanent gold corners;
  - tap-to-cancel "works correctly", with no move spent;
  - selection was comfortable at centre, edge and corner cells.
- **S23 device matrix (Step 11, ADB touch):**
  - the cue appears on release, is retained on a distant tap and clears on
    cancel;
  - it is absent in sampled move frames;
  - no cue remains after a win or loss.

Record: `Docs/Validation/PHASE15_SUMMARY.md`.

Phase 18 may restyle the cue; it should not change these rules.
