Atik Gameworks · Galaxplode

Privacy Policy

Android package: com.tugaykapucu.match3

Effective date: To be confirmed when this policy is published.

This policy explains how Atik Gameworks handles information in connection with Galaxplode, our Android puzzle game, and how relevant platform services handle information separately.

Information used by the game

Galaxplode does not require an account. The current game has no advertising, in-app purchases, chat or social features. Atik Gameworks does not operate a game server that receives your saved progress.

The game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, photos or location to play. Unity's optional analytics and diagnostic-reporting services are disabled in this release.

Saved progress and Android backup

Galaxplode stores a progression number in the app's preferences on your device. This allows Continue to open your next level, or Play Again after you finish all levels. It does not save an unfinished board or the moves made during the current attempt.

Atik Gameworks does not upload this saved progression to its own server. Android's backup or device-transfer features may include app preferences, depending on your device and settings. Your saved progression may therefore be backed up or restored by Android.

Google Play licensing and security

Google Play performs licensing and installer checks for the version of Galaxplode distributed through Google Play. These checks communicate with the Play Store, which handles its platform processing under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play's information handling is separate from the game's local progression storage. This policy does not mean that Google collects no information when you use Google Play or Android.

Retention and deletion

The saved progression remains in the app's local preferences until the app's data is cleared or removed. You can clear Galaxplode's app data through Android Settings. This permanently resets the local progression.

Uninstalling removes local app data, but Android backup or transfer may restore previously saved preferences when the app is installed again. Manage platform backups through your device or Google account settings. Atik Gameworks cannot delete backups held by those services.

There is no Galaxplode account to delete. If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. We retain correspondence only as needed to address the request and meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

Intended audience

Galaxplode is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older. It is not designed or marketed for children under 13. The game does not ask players for their date of birth.

A parent or guardian can contact Atik Gameworks with privacy questions or concerns about a child's information. Please avoid including a child's full name, address or other unnecessary personal information in your message. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

This privacy-policy webpage

This page does not include advertising, tracking scripts, analytics, forms or embedded third-party content.

This webpage is intended for hosting on tugaykapucu.com using Vercel. When you visit, Vercel may process technical request information, such as your IP address and browser or system information, to deliver and secure the webpage. This website handling is separate from the game's saved progression. See Vercel's Privacy Notice for information about Vercel's own processing.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our information practices change. Any future advertising, analytics, online features or other changes to information handling require a policy update.
